Steps to read your card

  1. 1Connect a contactless smart card reader to your PC.
  2. 2Install the DzaEidCard plugin from the ministry portal.
  3. 3Click "Open Official Reader Portal".
  4. 4Place your card on the reader and wait for the data.
  5. 5Copy the data and return here to continue.
⚠️ Note: Reading the chip requires a physical reader and the official plugin.
